About AMAL

Alvest Millennium Aviation Leasing IFSC Private Limited (AMAL) is a joint venture incorporated between the Alvest Group, France and Millennium Aero Dynamics, India.

AMAL is incorporated as an IFSCA regulated company at Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City) SEZ.

The company intends to provide innovative Ground Support Equipment products and services to meet the operational, environmental and commercial needs of Airline, Ground Handling & Airport Companies in the domestic and international markets.

Alvest Group and Millennium Aero Dynamics create a JV to expand GSE offering in India

The partnership extends a three-decade long relationship and signifies growing demand within the Indian market.

The Alvest Group and Millennium Aero Dynamics have created a joint venture to expand their Ground Support Equipment (GSE) offering in the dynamic Indian subcontinental market. The partnership extends the three-decade long relationship between the two companies and signifies growing demand within the Indian market.

Alvest Millennium Aviation Leasing IFSC Private Limited (AMAL) is incorporated as an IFSCA regulated company at Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City) SEZ.

The company intends to provide innovative Ground Support Equipment products and services to meet the operational, environmental and commercial needs of Airline, Ground Handling & Airport Companies in the domestic and international markets.

AMAL will provide environmentally-friendly aircraft ground support equipment under a fully serviced operational lease, giving customers an option for equipment with zero CAPEX so they can focus on their operations without worrying about downtime.

BlogLeasing Insights

Zero CAPEX: how a fully serviced GSE operational lease works

Why airlines, ground handlers and airports are choosing to lease ground support equipment instead of buying it.

Buying ground support equipment ties up significant capital and brings the ongoing responsibility of maintaining it. A fully serviced operational lease changes that model.

Under AMAL's operational lease, the customer gets the equipment it needs without upfront capital expenditure. Maintenance and technical support are included in the lease, so the equipment stays in service and the customer can focus on its operations instead of worrying about downtime.

The lease is designed for Airline, Ground Handling and Airport Companies in India and in international markets, and covers environmentally-friendly equipment that supports operators' environmental goals.
