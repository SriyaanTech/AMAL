About Us

A Joint Venture of Two Established Aviation Partners

Alvest Millennium Aviation Leasing IFSC Private Limited (AMAL) was incorporated in 2021 as a joint venture between TLD Group S.A.S. (France) and Millennium Aero Dynamics Private Limited (India).

Alvest and Millennium have been exclusive partners for over 30 years and have worked together to provide a broad range of services. Building on that long association, they have come together to provide leasing solutions for aircraft parts and GSE at GIFT, Gandhinagar, for the aviation industry.

Alvest Group · TLD

France

The largest manufacturer of aviation Ground Support Equipment (GSE) in the world. The range includes ground power systems, aircraft pushback systems, aircraft air-conditioning units, cargo loaders, baggage tractors and specialised military aircraft systems.

Millennium Aero Dynamics

India

Marketing, commissioning, warranty management, maintenance, repair, overhaul and refurbishment services for GSE in India and abroad, delivered 24×7 by a team of over 700 engineers and technicians at more than 40 airports in India.

Our Services

Leasing Solutions for the Aviation Industry

AMAL offers lease structures suited to the operational and financial requirements of airlines, ground handling agencies and airports in India and overseas.

Operating Lease

Fully serviced operating leases of ground support equipment, including maintenance and technical support, without upfront capital expenditure.

Financial and Hybrid Lease

Lease structures that provide a path to asset ownership, in line with the IFSCA framework for aircraft lease.

Aircraft Parts Leasing

Leasing of aircraft parts to domestic and international operators from GIFT City.

Maintenance and Asset Management

Commissioning, warranty management, maintenance, repair, overhaul and refurbishment through Millennium's service network.

Why Lease from GIFT IFSC

Regulatory Framework

Aircraft ground support equipment is a notified financial product under the IFSCA framework for aircraft lease.

Tax Incentives

Units in the IFSC are eligible for tax benefits that support competitive leasing from India.

Domestic and Cross-Border Leasing

Leasing to Indian operators as well as international airlines and ground handlers.

Global Manufacturer, Local Support

Equipment from a global manufacturer, supported by an established service team in India.
